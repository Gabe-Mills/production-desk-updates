Production Desk

Local film scheduling software tool made by some friends at Squid Productions to help make scheduling and script breakdowns easier.

Mac

Download Production Desk 1.4.2 for Mac

For Apple Silicon Macs running macOS 12 or later. Unzip the download and move Production Desk.app into your Applications folder. Existing projects are preserved when replacing the app.

Already installed? Choose Production Desk → Check for Updates… in the app.

This personal build is not Apple-notarized. macOS may require first-launch approval.

iPhone and iPad

Free, offline screenplay breakdowns, schedules, shot lists and reports. Same Production Desk, made for touch.

Download iPhone / iPad setup for Mac

Development preview for iOS 17 or later. Requires a Mac with Xcode and an existing Apple Development signing setup for your registered device. Unzip on your Mac and open Prepare Production Desk.command. The app builds and signs locally; choose your device to install.

This is a Mac setup download. iPhone Safari cannot install it directly. Apple may require Developer Mode and trust on your device; macOS may request approval for the unsigned setup script. It contains no signing credentials or provisioning profiles.
