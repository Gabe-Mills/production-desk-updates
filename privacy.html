Production Desk privacy policy

For Production Desk 1.4.1 on iPhone and iPad. This policy describes the iOS app, rather than the Mac app or this website.

Local work, without an account

You do not need an account to use the app. Screenplay parsing, breakdowns, scheduling, shot planning and report creation happen on your device. This iOS version does not provide Google account linking or automatic cloud synchronization.

The app does not automatically send your production data, usage information or device identifiers to the developer. It has no advertising, analytics, tracking or third-party SDKs, and does not collect data for the developer through its normal operation.

What the app stores

The app stores the work you import or enter, including script text, production details, cast and other elements, notes, schedules and shot lists, in its local workspace. It keeps a previous workspace copy and may preserve damaged files for recovery. Your appearance preference is also stored locally.

You can access workspace files in Files → On My iPhone or iPad → Production Desk. The app reads the size of its workspace files to check that they can be opened safely; it does not use file metadata to identify or track you.

Sharing and other services

You choose whether to export a backup, USS file, CSV or PDF, save it to Files, share it with another app, or print it. Those actions give the selected destination or service access to the exported information. A destination you choose may store or transmit it under its own privacy policy.

Files you import may come from iCloud Drive or another file provider. Your device's backup settings may also place app data in an iCloud or computer backup. These are services you manage through your device or provider; the app does not operate its own cloud storage service.

Opening an external link or sending a support email uses another app or service. If you email support, the message, your email address and any attachments you choose to send are shared with the support recipient. Include only information you are comfortable sharing.

Your choices and retained copies

You can edit or remove scenes, production elements and shots in the app. Older information may remain in the previous workspace or recovery files, or in backups and exports you have already made. Manage those copies separately if you want to remove them.

You can manage local workspace files through Files while Production Desk is closed. Keep a full backup before replacing or removing files you still need. Deleting the app removes its on-device app data; exported files and device or cloud backups are managed separately and may remain.
